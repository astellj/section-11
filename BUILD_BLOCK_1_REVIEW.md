## 5. Structural Findings (for next-block design)

1. **Prescribe off eFTP/MMP, retest at block start** with a less inflationary protocol than the 20-min test. Threshold at 91–95% of an honest number turns failed sessions into completed ones (3 Jul proved it).
2. **Map the life calendar before designing the block.** Skizzy Mars (19 Jun), heatwave + Overmono (23–26 Jun), Italy (15–21 Jul), Tramlines (24 Jul), Chris Stussy (31 Jul), Center Parcs (10 Aug) were all knowable at plan time. Pin deload weeks to disrupted weeks; don't lose build weeks unplanned.
3. **Progress one variable at a time on threshold** — 2×18@97% → 2×20@98% raised duration and intensity together and failed. Extend time at held intensity, then nudge intensity.
4. **Control the ramp.** Week 1→2 went 7.1h → 14.7h (ACWR ~1.77) and produced the late-May overreach (feel 4–5, RPE-9 VO₂ 2 Jun, knee niggle + threshold failure 4 Jun). Target steady +3–5 TSS/week CTL ramp, not the 555 → 248 → 512 → 45 sawtooth.
5. **Long-ride cornerstone never progressed:** mean ~2.5h, max 3.9h, no 4–5h training rides before a 100km race (and a 100-miler on the calendar). Winter/spring blocks must actually progress long-ride duration.
6. **Honour the auto-deload triggers** — treat two-trigger mornings as non-negotiable, especially before big rides.
7. **Weekend double long rides don't work** (30–31 May evidence). One long ride + one genuinely easy day.
8. **Book anchor events early** — mid-block motivation eroded partly because no event was dated until late.
9. **Keep:** VO₂ progression model (reps lengthen, then intensity), strength Tue/Thu, long-ride cornerstone slot, race-fuelling practice (~87g/h validated), planned deloads and post-disruption light re-entry sessions.
