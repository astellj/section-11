## 3. Why This Training

A ~2-minute maximal effort is roughly **60% aerobic / 40% anaerobic**. Two consequences:

1. **Anaerobic capacity (W′) is the biggest untapped lever.** The last 12 months contain
   essentially zero anaerobic work — the training history is Z2, threshold and VO₂max. It is
   the fastest-adapting system in cycling and it has never been trained.
2. **MAP / VO₂max is the aerobic ceiling.** 42-day 5-min MMP is **281 W (4.1 W/kg)**. Build Block 1
   proved a clear response to VO₂ work (5×5 min at 277–284 W was the standout session of the block).

**What is deliberately absent:** threshold, sweet spot, and long VO₂ intervals. Wrong systems,
and they cost the freshness this event needs. FTP is not a target in this block.

**Prescription basis:** 42-day MMP and eFTP (240 W), **not** the stored 252 W FTP, which is
55 days stale and was shown in the Build Block 1 review to be inflated.

after strength. Long ride Sunday, weather permitting.

**Volume policy.** This is a peaking block, not a crash diet on aerobic fitness. Weeks 1–3 carry
**6.7–7.9 h of riding plus ~2.6 h of strength**, with the aerobic hours going in as genuine Z2 —
90-minute Wednesdays and longer ride-out/ride-home either side of the quality sessions. Volume at

(~155 bpm)*. CTL still climbs across the block rather than sitting flat: **49.5 → 57.1 peak → 51.7 on race
day**, a +2.5/week ramp, inside the +3–5 target from the Build Block 1 review. **Week 4 is the one
exception** and must not be extended; the taper is the point.

## 5. Session Library

### Q1 — MAP / 30-15s *(Tue 15, Tue 22 · indoor or outdoor)*

Rønnestad short intervals — the highest-yield way to lift a VO₂ ceiling in three weeks.

- **Warm-up** 15′ building, including 3 × 30″ at ~300 W with 1′ easy.
- **Main:** 3 sets × 13 × (**30″ at 310–330 W** / 15″ easy). **3′ easy between sets.**
- **Cool-down** 10′.
- Total ~75–80′.

Targets are ~110–118% of the 281 W 5-min MMP. Fading slightly by set 3 is expected and fine —
hold ≥295 W rather than chasing the top of the range. ERG off, as usual.

### Q2 — Specific hill reps *(Thu 17, Thu 24 · outdoor)*

- **Warm-up** 20′ riding out, including 2 × 1′ builds and 2 × 20″ standing starts.
- **Thu 17:** 4 × 2:00 at **355–365 W**. Comfortable cadence.
  Deliberately stand for the final 30″ of every rep.
  *(Ridden 17 Sep at 368 / 357 / 355 / 360 W — correct. The 380/395 W originally written here was
  race power, not rep power, and was unreachable.)*
- **Thu 24:** 4 × 2:00 **in race gear, from a dead stop**, at **355 W** (141% — on the calendar).
  Final rep all-out, and ride it as a **hard start** — this is the pacing A/B that 12 Sep missed.
- **Recovery: 6–8 min between reps** — descend plus a loop at the bottom. Long recoveries are
  deliberate: this is one maximal effort on the day, not a repeatability event. Quality per rep
  beats accumulated volume.
- **Cool-down** riding home.

**Hill selection criteria, in priority order:**
1. **Gradient must increase toward the end.** Monsal is back-loaded; a hill that eases at the top
   trains the wrong pacing instinct. This matters more than the average gradient.
2. 2:00–2:30 at full effort, averaging ~9–11%.
3. No junctions, gates or give-ways inside the effort.
4. Safe and quick to descend.

A ~10-minute climb works fine — use its bottom two minutes (or whichever 2′ section ramps up)
and turn around.

The single highest-value session in the block. Everything downstream is calibrated off it.

1. Warm up 25–30′ on roads near Monsal Head; include 2 × 1′ builds and 2 × 20″ efforts.

## 6. Race Day — Sunday 4 October

### Bike setup

- **Small ring from the start line, and never touch the left lever.** The whole climb fits in the
  36: roughly 36×12 at the bottom → 36×18 mid → 36×22 upper → **36×32 on the final ramp (~64 rpm
  at 400 W)**. A front shift under full load is how people drop chains and lose five seconds.
  Confirm the exact sequence on the recon.
- **Strip the bike.** No bottle — it's a two-minute effort. No saddle bag, no tools, no spare
  tube. ~0.5–1 kg, worth ~1 second. Free.
- Keep the head unit — the data is worth more than its 60 g.

### Warm-up (start 45 min before your allotted start time)

*Not on the calendar — the race event is marked as the effort only. Finalise this nearer the day
once the start sheet is out and the recon has told us where there is road to warm up on.*

1. 15′ easy building.
2. 3 × 1′ at ~240 W, 1′ easy between.
3. 2 × 30″ at race intensity (~400 W), 2′ easy between.
4. 1 × 20″ standing start in race gear.
5. 8–10′ easy, finishing **10–12 min before your start**.
6. **Stay warm** — jacket on, keep the legs turning. Early October in the Peak District.

### Pacing — the thing that decides the race

**Hard 10–15 seconds off the line**, then **settle slightly below goal average through the
shallow bottom**, then **build into the ramp and empty it in the final 40 seconds.**

Two independent reasons, both pointing the same way:

- **Physiological.** A 20% pitch with nowhere to hide punishes anyone who arrives over the edge.
  Grinding at ~64 rpm means high pedal force, more muscular occlusion and a faster burn-through
  of W′ than the same watts at 90 rpm — the last 40 seconds costs more than the numbers suggest.
- **Physical.** On the shallow bottom you're at ~30 km/h and a meaningful share of your power
  goes to air resistance. On the ramp you're at ~9 km/h and essentially every watt lifts you.
  **The same watt buys more time at the top than at the bottom.**

Standing is aerodynamically free at 9 km/h (~1 W). Stand whenever it helps you make power.

### Fuelling

Normal breakfast ~3 h before. No carb loading — it's a two-minute effort. Nothing special.

## 7. Rules & Guardrails

### Knee — the main way this block goes wrong

This block stacks heavy squats and split squats with maximal low-cadence, high-force,
out-of-saddle grinding, on a rider with a documented right-knee history (severe enough to have
stopped riding once; a niggle on 4 Jun 2026 ended a threshold session). Both loads land on the
same joint.

- **Hill reps build into race gear** — week 2 at a comfortable cadence, week 3 in race gear at
  race cadence. **No grinding in week 1.**
- **Any knee pain during a rep ends the session**, not just the rep.
- **No low-cadence work beyond what is prescribed.** Do not add torque efforts to warm-ups.

### Other

- **Readiness triggers from the dossier still apply.** Two-trigger mornings are non-negotiable —
  Build Block 1's finding #6.
- **A missed quality session is skipped, not stacked.** Never two hard days back to back.
- **Padel:** keep it off the day before a quality session, and out of race week entirely.
- **Strength unchanged** except Thu 1 Oct at half volume, stopping well short of failure.
- **Long rides are weather-dependent** — flag the weekends that look bad rather than forcing them.
