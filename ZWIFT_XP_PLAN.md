# Zwift XP Plan — levelling fastest per hour ridden

_Written 2026-09-23. Rider: 68.6 kg, 180 cm, FTP 252 W (eFTP ~240), Z2 = 141–189 W.
Weekly riding 8–12 h. Off-block until 4 Oct (Monsal Head), winter structured block from ~Nov._

> **The whole plan in one sentence:** ride every session on a route you haven't badged yet, in
> the mode that pays best for that terrain, in a robopacer group when there's nothing left to
> badge, and never miss the weekly freebies. Do that and a typical 10-hour week roughly doubles
> from ~6,000 XP to ~11,000–13,000 XP with **no change to the training**.

---

## 1. How XP actually works (verified Sept 2026)

| Mechanic | Rule | Notes / source date |
|---|---|---|
| Free ride, group ride, race, robopacer | **20 XP per km** (32/mile). Nothing for elevation. | Zwift Insider, May 2026 |
| Workout mode | Time-based, replaces distance XP. **Interval blocks 12 XP/min. Steady "zone" blocks 10/min. Warm-up/cool-down ramps and free-ride blocks 5–6/min.** Intensity is irrelevant: 1 min at Z1 = 1 min at Z6. Full XP needs the full star (≥88% of target; ERG guarantees it). No completion bonus. | Zwift Insider article dated 2020, still cited by their May 2026 levels guide and consistent with GPLama's 2022 test (70 min → 840 XP). **Re-verify on your first session** (§8). |
| Route badge (first completion of a route) | One-off bonus ≈ **20 XP per km of the route**, i.e. it doubles that ride. ~102 routes ≈ 102,000 XP available. | Counts in free ride, events, meetups **and workout mode**, as long as the route was selected from the menu and completed. Not doubled in double-XP events. |
| Arch power-ups | Small XP bonus **+10**, large **+250**. Large chance ~10% on a normal ride. **On a TT bike you cannot receive speed power-ups, so every arch pays the XP bonus instead.** | Zwift Insider, Sept 2026 |
| Week streak | One ride ≥2 km per calendar week: **300 → 400 → 500 XP/week** from week 3 on. Outdoor rides keep the streak alive but don't pay. | Zwift Insider |
| Route of the Week / Climb of the Week | **250 or 500 XP each** (some weeks pay drops instead). One completion, any mode, resets Monday 17:00 UK. Cards on the home screen. | Zwift Insider, Sept 2026 |
| Alpe du Zwift summit spinner | **+250 XP** for any prize you already own, **+1,000** if it lands on Meilenstein wheels you already own. First-time prizes are items, not XP. | |
| Climb Portal | Only XP is from its 10 arches per climb. Large-bonus chance was cut in v1.92 (July 2025): best case now ~**1,600 XP/h** at 3 W/kg on Oude Kwaremont at 100%. Was ~3,900. | Zwift Insider, July 2025 |
| Outdoor rides synced to Zwift | 5 XP/km, capped at 1,000 XP/week. Free money, but small. | |
| Levels | Cumulative XP: L10 11k · L20 33.5k · L25 48.5k · L30 68.5k · L35 95k · L40 127.5k · L45 166.5k · L50 212k · L60 310.5k. Level 100 ≈ 591k; unlimited beyond (20k/level). | Zwift Insider, May 2026 |

**Dead or not worth it:** weight/height games (against the ToS and detected server-side), Climb Portal farming (nerfed), the "61-second block" workout quirk (worth ~1 XP), Everesting badge (5,000 XP for 8,848 m in one ride).

## 2. Your XP-per-hour ladder

All figures are for you at Z2 unless stated. "Arches" assumes ~4 per hour on flat Watopia loops (expected value ≈ 34 XP each).

| # | How you ride the hour | Effort | XP/hour | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Solo Z2 free ride, road bike, flat | ~165 W | ~640 | ~32 km/h × 20 |
| 2 | Workout mode, steady zone blocks | any | 600 | 10/min |
| 3 | **Workout mode built only from interval blocks** | any | **720** | 12/min, terrain irrelevant |
| 4 | Solo Z2 on the **TT bike**, flat | ~170 W | ~730 | ~34–35 km/h + guaranteed arch XP |
| 5 | **Coco robopacer group** (2.6 W/kg, ~38 km/h) | ~160–170 W in the draft | **~890** | 760 distance + arches |

| 7 | Jacques group (3.2 W/kg, ~42 km/h) | ~205–215 W (tempo) | ~970 | Not a Z2 ride |
| 8 | Alpe workout combo (§4c), repeat visit | Z2 | ~900–1,000 | 720/h up + spinner + fast descent |
| 9 | ZRacing / mass-start race, flat | hard | ~900–1,100 + stage bonuses | 40+ km/h, and a quality session |
| 10 | **Un-badged flat route, TT bike, free ride** | Z2 | **~1,400** | #4 + the badge |
| 11 | **Un-badged hilly route in workout mode** | any | **~1,100–1,300** | 720/h + badge; slowness on climbs costs nothing in workout mode |
| 12 | **Volcano Circuit × 25 in one ride** (§4a) | Z2, ~3 h | **~2,000** | 3,000 XP of lap badges on top of everything |
| 13 | Anything on a Tour of Watopia route, 5 Oct–22 Nov | any | **×2 on the distance XP** | see §5 |

The two rules that fall out of the table:

- **Terrain decides the mode.** Flat un-badged route → free ride on the TT bike (speed pays). Hilly un-badged route → workout mode (time pays, gradient is free).
- **Group beats solo only once the badges are gone.** Coco (~890/h) loses to any un-badged route (~1,100–1,400/h). Robopacers ride a fixed rotation, so they're the fallback for Z2 hours, not the plan.

## 3. The standing rules (every ride, every week)

1. **Never ride a badged route when an un-badged one fits the session.** Keep the Zwift Insider route list open and tick routes off. Do flat ones as TT free rides, hilly ones as workouts.
2. **Every structured session is a workout built only from interval blocks.** No ramp warm-ups, no steady blocks, no free-ride blocks. In intervals.icu, write the warm-up as a two-step repeat (e.g. `5x 1m 50% 1m 60%`) instead of a ramp, and long steady Z2 as `10x 5m 65% 1m 68%` instead of one 60-min block. Same training, +20–140% XP on those minutes. Keep ERG on so the stars are automatic.
3. **Point every workout at an un-badged route that you'll finish inside the session.** ~30–35 km covered per hour on flat routes at Z2 in workout mode, ~15–20 km/h on the big climbs. If the workout ends before the route does, keep riding in free ride until the badge pops.
4. **Unstructured Z2 with no badge left to take = Coco (or Yumi if it's a good day).** Watopia only from Sept 2026, flatter routes, same routes Mon–Thu and Fri–Sun. Sit in the pack, don't pull.
5. **Monday admin (2 minutes):** check the Route of the Week and Climb of the Week cards, and fold whichever one fits into that week's rides. Streak needs one 2 km ride per week: never let it lapse (streak savers only exist after 12 weeks).
6. **Drops go on speed, in this order:** best TT frame at your level → disc/deep wheels (DT Swiss ARC 1100 disc or Zipp 858/Super9) → road frame. Set the **Climb Mt. Everest** challenge active if it isn't; every Alpe rep counts toward the Tron bike.
7. **One "haul ride" per fortnight is the long ride** (§4). These are 3–5 h Z2 rides, which is what the winter cornerstone ride already is.
8. **Sprint badges once, after 4 Oct:** one session with a few max sprints picks up 500/600/700 W (+ Jelly 500 W × 10 s) for ~400 XP. Descents on the Alpe/Ven-Top opportunistically pick up the 48/64/80 km/h speed badges (250/500/750) and 100 km/h (1,000).

## 4. The haul rides (long-ride slot)

**a) Volcano Circuit × 25 — the best single ride in the game for you.**
Route "Volcano Circuit" (4.1 km, ~20 m/lap). 25 laps ≈ 103 km ≈ 3 h at Z2 on the TT bike.
Yield: 2,060 distance + **3,000 lap badges (5/10/25, all in one activity)** + 500 for the 100 km badge if you don't have it + ~850 from 25 arches on the TT bike + the route badge ≈ **6,000–6,500 XP ≈ 2,000/h**. Do this first.

**b) The pretzels — big one-off badges, ridden in workout mode because they're hilly.**
Mega Pretzel 107 km (+2,140), Über Pretzel 128 km (+2,560), Four Horsemen 89 km (+1,780), Big Foot Hills 67 km (+1,340). As 4–5 h Z2 interval-block workouts: ~720/h + badge ≈ 1,150–1,250/h, and they're proper long rides.

**c) Alpe workout combo (GPLama's method).**
Route "Road to Sky". Workout of interval blocks only, sized to your climb time (Z2 at ~170 W ≈ 75 min up). At the summit end the workout, take the spinner, free-ride the descent (~12 km at 60–80 km/h ≈ 11 min ≈ 240 XP, plus speed badges first time). First visit ≈ 1,800 XP in ~100 min including the route badge; repeats ≈ 1,500. Each summit also progresses Avid Climber (5 → 500 XP), Masochist (25 → 1,000) and the Tron bike.

**d) Climb Portal, only on tempo days.** ~1,600/h needs ~3 W/kg (≈205 W). Legs of Steel (25 climbs) = 1,000 XP, Pro (10) = 500. Use it when a session is tempo anyway, and for Climb of the Week.

## 5. Calendar

**Now → Sun 4 Oct (race week).** The hill-climb plan says no added volume, so nothing here adds time:
- Party Crashers group rides run to 4 Oct with a **1-in-3 chance of +250 XP at every arch** (expected ≈ +90/arch). If a scheduled easy indoor ride matches a stage length (Stage 4, 29 Sep–4 Oct: Island Hopper, 31 km), do it as a **Category D "partier"** ride at Z2 instead of a plain free ride. Not Fri/Sat, and only at genuinely easy pace.
- Set up: TT bike and wheels bought, Everest challenge active, badge audit done, warm-ups in your intervals.icu templates rewritten as two-step repeats.

**Mon 5 Oct → Sun 22 Nov: Tour of Watopia, double XP.** Your harvest window, and it starts the day after the race.
- Five stages, each completable as an event **or on demand**. Double XP applied to distance XP on tour routes on demand last year (Zwift Unlocked), and workout XP doubled on those routes too. **Ride every session on a ToW stage route until it's confirmed either way** (§8), then keep doing it.
- Stage completion bonuses (last year: 1,000 XP for 3 stages, kit for 5) and a new "ultimate challenge" — do all five stages, ideally as the on-demand ride you were doing anyway.
- **Mon 26 Oct: Watopia expansion opens (Stage 4) with ~20 new routes**, i.e. ~20 fresh badges including a flat bypass around the Epic KOM and two crit circuits. Badge the flat ones on the TT bike, the hilly ones as workouts, inside the double-XP window.
- Volcano × 25 (§4a) goes in the first weekend after the race, or the first weekend it isn't a ToW route (badges aren't doubled, so it doesn't need the window).

**23 Nov onward: winter block.** Nothing changes except the mode per session:
- Tue/Thu quality → interval-block workouts on un-badged routes (or a ZRacing race: ~2,000 XP of stage bonuses per monthly series, and it is the quality session).
- Wed/Sun Z2 → un-badged flat route on the TT bike until none are left, then Coco.
- Saturday long ride → a haul ride (§4) every other week, otherwise the biggest un-badged route that fits.
- Power Play campaign 23 Nov–27 Dec: check its XP terms when announced.

## 6. What a week yields

Winter-block week, 10 h, badges still available:

| Session | Mode | XP |
|---|---|---|
| 2 × 75-min quality | interval-block workout on un-badged route | 2 × ~1,400 = 2,800 |
| 2 × 90-min Z2 | un-badged flat, TT bike | 2 × ~2,100 = 4,200 |
| 4-h long ride | hilly un-badged route in workout mode | ~4,700 |
| 60-min recovery | interval-block workout (any route) | 720 |
| Weekly freebies | streak + RotW/CotW | ~1,000 |
| **Total** | | **~13,400** (vs ~6,000 riding the same hours on default routes) |

Once the badges are exhausted (~8–12 weeks of this) the same week settles at ~9,000–10,000 XP. At 12k/week: level 25 → 30 in under 2 weeks, → 40 in ~7 weeks, → 50 in ~14 weeks.

## 7. Things that will get you banned or waste time

- Changing weight or height to anything but the real numbers. Zwift locks them in events and shadow-bans on the server. Not worth a single level.
- Mid-ride weight/bike-swap tricks: patched in 2025.
- "Just Watch", unsaved rides, dropped connections: no XP. Save every ride.
- Relying on climb-portal RNG or 1-second workout blocks. Both are gone or negligible.

---

## 8. Verify on first use (each is a 30-second check)

1. **Workout XP rates.** Note XP before and after your first interval-block workout. Expect 12 × minutes. If it's lower, the 2020 rule has changed and rule 2 gets dropped.
2. **Badge in workout mode.** Confirm the badge pops when you complete a route inside a workout.
3. **ToW on-demand double XP.** After the first on-demand stage-route ride, check that distance XP was 40/km. If it's 20, ride the stages as events instead.
4. **intervals.icu → Zwift block mapping.** Open the pushed workout in Zwift: a two-step repeat should show as an interval block, not a series of steady blocks. If it flattens, build the workout in Zwift's editor instead.
